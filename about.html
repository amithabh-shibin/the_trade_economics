About the Academy

Financial Education Built Around Discipline

The Trade Economics helps learners build a foundation in market mechanics, risk management and trading psychology.

Meet the Founder

Savio Menezes

Savio founded The Trade Economics in 2023. His 20 years in banking include work as a traded risk manager and analyst in global markets.

His background informs the academy’s structured approach to market mechanics, risk and trading psychology.

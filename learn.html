Market Mechanics

How Trades Take Place

Buyers and sellers submit orders through brokers and platforms. When eligible orders match, a trade is executed. Learning how a market handles those orders helps explain what a price chart records.

Technical Analysis

Reading a Candlestick Chart

A candlestick records the opening, closing, highest and lowest prices in a period. A series of candles shows how prices changed over time. The Advanced programme explores candlesticks, charts and price action.

View related programme →
Order Flow

What Trading Activity Can Tell You

Order flow describes buying and selling activity as it occurs. Looking at that activity alongside the chart can help you describe how the market behaved at a price level. Real-time breakdowns are among the topics covered in Mentorship.

Risk & Psychology

Thinking About Risk and Decisions

Risk management starts by defining what you could lose. Trading psychology examines how emotions and habits influence decisions. The academy covers both topics in its learning framework.

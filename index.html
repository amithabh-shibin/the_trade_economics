FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Ask us about programmes, mentorship or joining the community.

Email Us
Do I need trading experience?

The Beginner programme starts with financial markets, stocks and market mechanics. You can start without prior trading experience.

What does the Advanced programme cover?

It covers trading psychology, an introduction to Nasdaq, charts and candlesticks, price action, indicators, strategy, and risk and trade management.

What is included in Mentorship?

Mentorship provides structured guidance from market fundamentals to advanced concepts. It includes live market application and real-time order flow breakdowns.

Is there a learning community?

The academy describes peer networks, daily chatrooms, group accountability and events. Contact the academy for current access details.

How do I ask about fees and schedules?

Email the academy with the programme you are interested in and a short summary of your experience. The team can explain current fees, availability and next steps.

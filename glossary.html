The Trading Glossary

Plain-language definitions of terms you’ll encounter in the academy’s programmes.

Candlestick

A chart mark that shows the opening, closing, high and low prices for a selected time period.

Fundamental Analysis

An approach that examines information such as a company’s earnings, finances and business conditions to understand its value.

Market Mechanics

The processes by which orders are placed, matched and executed in a market.

Order Flow

The sequence and volume of buying and selling activity as it occurs in a market.

Price Action

The movement of price over time, typically studied directly on a chart.

Risk Management

The process of identifying and setting limits on potential losses.

Trade Management

The decisions involved in overseeing an open position, including adjustments and exits.

Trading Psychology

The study of how emotions, habits and biases can influence trading decisions.
